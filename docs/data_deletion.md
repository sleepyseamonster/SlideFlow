## How to Request Deletion of Your SlideFlow + Meta Data

SlideFlow lets you remove connected Meta accounts at any time and request full data deletion.

1. **Disconnect Meta inside SlideFlow**
   - Sign in at https://yourdomain.com.
   - Go to **Profile → Posting destinations**.
   - Click **Disconnect** next to each Meta destination (Instagram Business account / Facebook Page) you want removed.

2. **Request full deletion (optional)**
   - Email **[support@placeholder.com]** with the subject “Meta Data Deletion Request.”
   - Include the email address tied to your SlideFlow account and (optionally) the Instagram usernames or Facebook Page names you want removed.

3. **We confirm and delete**
   - Once we verify your request, we delete the associated Meta tokens, connection records, and (if requested) your SlideFlow account data, subject to our legal/operational retention requirements (e.g., billing, security logs, backups).
   - We will acknowledge the request and complete the deletion within a reasonable timeframe.

If you cannot access your SlideFlow account, email us from the address associated with the account and include any details that help verify ownership.
