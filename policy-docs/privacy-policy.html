## 1. Scope
This Privacy Policy applies to:
- The SlideFlow web application and related services.
- SlideFlow’s integration with Meta Platforms, Inc. products (Facebook Login and Instagram Graph API) used to connect accounts and publish content.

This Privacy Policy does not cover Meta’s own privacy practices. Review Meta’s policies separately.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information you provide to SlideFlow
- **Account information:** email address, name (and any other profile fields you choose to provide).
- **Content you upload or create:** images/videos you upload, carousel titles, slide ordering, captions you write, and scheduling preferences.
- **Support communications:** messages you send us (e.g., emails or in-app requests).

### B. Information from Meta when you click “Connect to Meta”
When you connect Meta through SlideFlow, you authorize SlideFlow to access information needed to identify eligible assets and publish content. Depending on the permissions granted and your account configuration, SlideFlow may access and process:
- **Facebook Pages you manage** (Page id + Page name).
- **Instagram professional account information** connected to those Pages (Instagram business account id + username).
- **Meta access tokens** required to make Graph API requests and publish content (including exchanging a short-lived token for a long-lived token).

SlideFlow does **not** receive your Meta password.

### C. Payment information
If you subscribe, payments are processed by **Stripe**. SlideFlow may store limited billing identifiers (Stripe customer/subscription ids and subscription status). Card details are handled by Stripe, not stored by SlideFlow.

### D. Technical and usage information
We and our service providers may collect IP address, device/browser information, timestamps, and logs related to authentication, security, and app performance.

## 3. How We Use Information
We use information to:
- Provide and operate the Service (create accounts, store media, manage carousels, render previews, and enable publishing workflows).
- Connect your Meta account and identify eligible Instagram Business accounts / Facebook Pages.
- Publish your selected content to Instagram/Facebook **only when you initiate publishing or scheduling**.
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot, and improve reliability.
- Provide customer support and respond to requests.
- Manage subscriptions and enforce usage limits/quotas.

## 4. Meta Data Use, Storage, and Sharing

### A. What SlideFlow stores from Meta
To support publishing, SlideFlow stores:
- **Non-secret connection identifiers** shown in-app (Instagram professional account id/username, Facebook Page id/name).
- **Tokens needed for publishing** (stored server-side in Supabase `connected_account_secret`, not exposed to other users).

### B. How SlideFlow uses Meta data
SlideFlow uses Meta data and tokens to:
- Fetch Pages and Instagram Business accounts you can publish to.
- Publish your approved content via the Instagram Graph API.
- Maintain the connection, support default destination selection, and audit publishing attempts.

### C. Sharing with Meta
When publishing, SlideFlow sends requests to Meta’s APIs that may include:
- Instagram business account id / Page id.
- Media URLs or references needed by Meta to fetch and publish your content.
- Captions you provide.
- Access tokens required by the API.

This sharing is necessary to perform the actions you request.

## 5. How We Share Information (Service Providers)

- **Workflow/automation services** invoked via Supabase Edge Functions for ingestion, media derivatives, or publishing tasks.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transaction (e.g., merger/acquisition).
